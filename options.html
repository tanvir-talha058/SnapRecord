About

SnapRecord — Version 1.1.0

A lightweight screen recording extension for Chromium-based browsers.

  • Record tabs, windows, or the entire screen
  • Video quality up to 4K
  • System audio and microphone support
  • Camera overlay and on-screen drawing tools
  • Pause and resume recordings
